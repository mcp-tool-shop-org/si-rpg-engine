# Dispatch E4 — verbs beyond sideways

2026-09-25. Coordinator: Claude. Builder: the builder seat. Reviewer: a different family, on a scratch clone, before merge. Depends on E2 and E3, merged at `d9f8b2a`. The plan row is E4 in `docs/PHASE-1.md`.

## What it is

Today every admitted intent does one thing: it drives the actor sideways at the rule's speed toward a point or a body's near face for a counted number of quanta. `move` and `push` differ only by their target kind. The world is three-dimensional and a character in it climbs, carries, sets down, and acts on what is near it. Each of those is a verb with a hand-authored predicate, a hazard suite, an effect on the record, and a test on the real tick. None of them is a game; each is a capability the record can hold and the hash covers.

## Pins

1. **A rule names its effect.** `IntentRule` gains `effect: 'drive' | 'climb' | 'carry' | 'release' | 'episode'`. A rule without one compiles as `drive`, so `move.json` and `push.json` are unchanged in bytes and every fixture on `main` replays. The compiler refuses an unknown effect and refuses a field the effect does not use. The tick resolves an admitted intent by its effect; the predicate for each effect is code in `packages/tick/predicates.js`, parameterized by the rule's numbers, as `drive` is today.
2. **A support query.** `world.supportAt(x, z, fromY)` returns the height of the highest top face under a point, from a static collider, in its local frame when it is rotated, from the heightfield, or from a body at rest, cast down from `fromY`; `null` when nothing is under the point. It is exact on f64, tested on an axis-aligned box, a rotated slab, a heightfield cell, and a resting body, and it is what `climb` and `release` stand on.
3. **`climb`.** Point target. The predicate admits when the ground distance to the target is within `maxDistance`; the rise from the actor's feet to the support at the target is greater than the controller's step height, 0.3, and at most the rule's `maxRise`; the actor's box standing on that support is clear of every collider and body; and two swept segments are clear with the actor's half-extents, up by the rise from the actor's pose, then across to the target. A rise of 0.3 or less is refused with the reason that `move` steps it. Resolution is kinematic and counted: the actor rises at `speed` for the quanta the rise takes, then crosses at `speed` for the quanta the ground distance takes, rounded up, bounded by `maxQuanta`. While it rises the solver applies no gravity and no snap to it: the driven flag the binary reads gains a lifted state, and a lifted kinematic takes its vertical velocity from the record.
4. **`carry`** (the verb file is `pick-up.json`; the effect is `carry`). Body target. The predicate admits when the target is a dynamic body that is asleep in the solver, not mid-action, within `maxDistance` of its near face with a clear path, and no larger than `maxHalfExtent` on any axis; the actor is not already carrying. `world.sleeping(id)` is read from the solver snapshot, not from a velocity threshold. Resolution takes the quanta the approach takes, and on the quantum it completes the body leaves the solver world and is carried: the actor's record gains `carrying`, the body's record gains `carriedBy`, and each quantum the carried body's pose is the actor's pose raised by the two half-heights, with zero velocity and identity orientation. The solver signature gains the carried mask beside the driven mask, so the world is rebuilt without the carried body; the warm-start cache resets with it, and that is in the hash.
5. **`release`** (the verb file is `drop.json`). Point target. The predicate admits when the actor is carrying, the point is within `maxDistance`, the support under the point exists, the carried body's box resting on that support is clear of every collider and body, and the path from the actor to the point is clear with the carried body's half-extents. On the quantum it completes the body re-enters the solver awake at rest on that support, `carrying` and `carriedBy` clear, and the signature changes back.
6. **`use`** (the effect is `episode`). Body or zone target: `targetKind` gains `'zone'`. For a body, the near face is within `maxDistance` with a clear path; for a zone, the actor's zone is the target zone. The world does not change. The effect is one admitted episode, `use <actor> <target>` at the tick, in the memory record, and the action occupies one quantum. This is the episode an E5 belief cites.
7. **The hash.** `carrying` and `carriedBy` are indices mixed as `u32` after each body's zone index. They are mixed only while some body is carried, so a frame with nothing carried hashes as it does today and every fixture on `main` stays a proof; the rule is stated in `packages/tick/tick.js` beside the zone rule. The carried mask is in the solver signature and therefore in the snapshot. The product scene gains a climb and a carry, so the golden covers both; `write-golden` rewrites `fixtures/golden.txt` once with the reason in the commit. The arithmetic golden does not move.
8. **Hazards are scoped by effect.** `predicates/hazards/index.json` becomes `{ scenarios: [{ file, effect }] }`, the four existing scenarios carry `drive`, and a draft runs the scenarios for its effect. A scenario may now name a `targetZone`, may mark a body `asleep`, and may state `carrying`. Each new effect ships at least two admitting and two refusing scenarios: `climb` refuses a rise past `maxRise`, refuses a blocked standing volume, refuses a rise the step height covers, and admits a ledge in reach; `carry` refuses a body past `maxHalfExtent`, refuses a body that is awake, refuses a body behind a wall, and admits a resting body in reach; `release` refuses a blocked volume, refuses when nothing is carried, and admits a clear point on a support; `episode` refuses a body out of reach, refuses a zone the actor is not in, and admits each in reach.
9. **The drafts and the catalog.** The four drafts are `fixtures/climb-draft.json`, `fixtures/pick-up-draft.json`, `fixtures/drop-draft.json`, and `fixtures/use-draft.json`. Each is admitted by `load admit` in the pull request, with the admitted rules under `predicates/intents` and the index naming six verbs. A test admits each draft from its file and shows one refused variant per draft.
10. **The fixture.** `fixtures/behavior-verbs.json` replays frame for frame on the product law: a climb onto a ledge of 0.8 that `move` refuses; a pick up, a carry across a gap the box could not cross on its own, and a drop on the far side; a use of a body and a use of a zone, each visible as an episode; and a refused climb, carry, drop, and use, each with its reason in the fixture.
11. **The binary changes once.** The driven flag gains the lifted state and the signature gains the carried mask. Digest pinned from the Linux build as E2 set it. The load pass through Rapier's collision pipeline from E3 is unchanged.
12. **The debug view** gains a verb mode: M is move, C climb, G pick up, D drop, U use, and a click targets a ground point, the nearest body in reach, or the actor's zone, as the verb wants. The current verb is shown beside the tick and the hash, and a carried body is drawn where the record puts it. Nothing else changes in it.
13. **The seat stays frozen.** Its grammar enumerates the admitted verbs as it does now and is not run. The host adapter is unchanged beyond the verb mode. `reachedGoal` is unchanged.
14. **The plan.** The E4 row in `docs/PHASE-1.md` gains one sentence naming the five effects and the carry limit of one body. No other prose changes.
